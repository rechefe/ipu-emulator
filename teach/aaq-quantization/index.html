IPU emulator · AaQ and XMEM

AaQ quantization course

Short lessons on how the IPU's AaQ stage quantizes a row, where the row's metadata and format live, and what the emulator needs to match the hardware spec.

Spec update

Master now has architecture specs for AaQ and MULT beside the microarchitecture spec. The course follows the architecture specs for everything a program can see, and lists the contradictions in the spec audit.

Lessons

  • 01Where a row's format lives: data bytes, metadata word, table configuration.
  • 02Tables, and how a load finds its table: table sets, contexts, the cache agent, the row address.
  • 03One E4: NX8: the hardware's only 8-bit format, and the old emulator E1–E7 it replaces.
  • 04Quantizing a row, step by step: the scale, the two branches, the two carry edges.
  • 05Dynamic tables: choosing E1M6 or E2M5 per row.
  • 06The activation LUT and the LUT load: 256 entries, five segments, and the hazards.
  • 07Back from XMEM: LD, MULT and expansion to FP32.
  • 08The emulator plan: old and new AaQ side by side, the PR sequence, and the open decisions.
